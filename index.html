About

I work where good learning design meets reliable delivery. I start with the learner, shape complex material into clear structures, and build the standards and workflows that keep quality consistent when production scales.

My path runs from classroom teaching and academic English facilitation into instructional design, learning quality assurance, and team leadership. I hold a Master of Education from Monash University and enjoy mentoring designers as much as designing courses.

Today I focus on eLearning development in Articulate Rise 360, learning quality systems, and the responsible use of AI in instructional design.
